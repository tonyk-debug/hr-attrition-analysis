## Business question

**Primary:** Which employee characteristics are most associated with attrition, so
that HR can identify at-risk profiles and focus retention efforts where they will
have the greatest impact?

*Target decision-maker: an HR Director allocating a limited retention budget.*

**Supporting questions:**
1. Which departments and job roles exceed the baseline attrition rate of 16.1%?
2. Are employees working overtime more likely to leave?
3. Is attrition related to career progression (tenure, time since last promotion)?

**Discarded:** the relationship between performance and attrition. The
`PerformanceRating` column contains only ratings of 3 and 4 — no poorly-rated
employees — which makes any conclusion about performance impossible.
